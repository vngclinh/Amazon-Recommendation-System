The Data

Two datasets, two lessons

We started on Amazon, hit a structural wall, and moved to Goodreads — which offers richer signals and a natural behavior chain.

🛒 Amazon Reviews 2018

  • Electronics + Home&Kitchen
  • ~14M reviews → 5.5M after 5-core
  • 609K users · 145K items
  • 99.99% sparse · heavy long-tail

Signal: ratings + review text only.

📚 Goodreads UCSD Book Graph focus

  • 15.7M reviews · 465K users · 2.08M books
  • Avg rating 3.77 / 5 · text on 100% of reviews
  • Rich signals: ratings, review length, votes, genres, shelves
  • Natural behavior chain: shelve → read → rate → recommend

Phase 1 · Amazon

A good RMSE that means nothing

✓ Rating prediction works

Ensemble ridge_positive: RMSE 1.1994 (−1.15% vs baseline). Weights B 57.5% · D 25% · Base 8.8% · C 8.8%.

⚠ But ranking fails

Recommending from all 143K items: Recall@10 ≈ 0.0001, coverage only 1.56%.

71% of items have ≤20 reviews → embeddings are noise. The model only re-ranks popular items.

→ The problem is the data structure, not the algorithm. This is why we moved to Goodreads.

Phase 2 · Goodreads — Data & EDA

What the data told us

Figure: review-length distribution & length by star rating

📈 Review length ↑ with rating

Users who liked a book write longer reviews → length is an engagement signal.

⭐ Skewed to high ratings

~69% are 4–5★ (mean 3.77) → must evaluate ranking, not just averages.

👍 Votes track popularity

Popular books get more votes regardless of personal fit → risk of popularity bias.

🌉 Bridge users

Readers spanning ≥3 genres connect communities (cross-genre signal).

Does the result survive full-ranking?

A shared evaluator scores ALS / chainRec / hybrid on the same test, pool & mask.

AUC ~0.95 with Recall@10 ~0.05 is consistent: with 1.57M items, even 0.95 leaves tens of thousands of items above the positive.

⚠ sampled@500 inflates

Review-length F3: +3.6% (sampled) → −0.8% AUC (full-ranking). Votes: −5.8% AUC.

ALS ≥ chainRec; hybrid ≈ ALS

Confirms the paper: Goodreads is unfavorable to chainRec.

Transfer limit

Review-length covers only 5.5% of chain edges → cannot weight chainRec.
